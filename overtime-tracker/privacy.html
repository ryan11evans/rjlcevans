Privacy Policy

New Deductions: OT + Car Loan. Effective October 29, 2026.

The home-screen name is New Deductions. This policy describes that iOS app.

Data stays on the device

The app stores what you enter in a local SwiftData database on the phone. That includes the tax profile (filing status, pay frequency, estimated MAGI, and the eligibility answers), the employer, workweeks, VINs, decoded vehicle fields, loan terms, any Form 1098-VLI interest you type, and any W-2 Box 12 code TT amount you type, including a mark that code TT is not on the W-2.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. It does not use iCloud. There is no advertising identifier and no tracker.

The only network call

The only request the app makes is to decode a VIN. When you tap Check VIN, and the check digit is valid, the app sends that 17-character VIN to the U.S. government's NHTSA vPIC API:

https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/api/vehicles/DecodeVinValues/{VIN}?format=json

It reads fields such as Plant Country from the response. The request does not include your name, tax figures, or loan numbers. If the lookup fails, the app stops. Nothing else leaves the phone to a server we operate.

Purchases

StoreKit purchases are handled by Apple. New Deductions Pro (product ID com.rjlcevans.newdeductions.pro) is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription. The app does not see your card number. Apple's payment system is separate from the VIN lookup.

Deleting data

Settings includes Reset onboarding, which deletes the tax profile, employer, weeks, and vehicles stored on the device. Removing the app deletes the local database as well.
