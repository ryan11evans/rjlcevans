Estimates only, not tax advice

The app estimates two federal Schedule 1-A figures for tax years 2025 through 2028: the FLSA overtime premium and qualified passenger-vehicle loan interest. Those numbers are estimates. They are not tax advice, not a tax return, and not tax-prep software. The app is not affiliated with the IRS. You are responsible for what you file. The overtime figure is only the half-time FLSA premium, not the full time-and-a-half paycheck amount.
